Good to know

        
The questions we keep getting

      

      

        

          
What should I wear?

          
Your dress code, in plain words — and a warning if the ground is grass, gravel or cobbles.

        

        

          
Can I bring the children?

          
Say yes or no here. It is kinder to be direct than to let people guess.

        

        

          
Can I bring someone?

          
Explain how plus-ones work — usually, whoever is named on the invitation.

        

        

          
What about gifts?

          
A registry link, a honeymoon fund, or a sentence saying your presence is enough.

        

        

          
Can I take photographs?

          
Whether the ceremony is unplugged, and any hashtag or shared album for the rest of the day.
